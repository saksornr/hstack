## Choosing the right ending

For a proposal, close with the evidence the study intends to produce and the limits of its interpretation. For a completed study, report the actual principal finding if supplied, including uncertainty where available. For a mixed-stage study, identify the pilot result as preliminary and describe the remaining evaluation prospectively.

## Choosing detail under a page limit

Keep details that answer “How will we know?” over details that merely inventory the project. For the video audit, condition matching, denominator definition, and model–provider separation are more useful than a long system list. For the persona study, blocked comparisons, blinded scoring, and separation of stereotypes from employment composition matter more than model branding.
